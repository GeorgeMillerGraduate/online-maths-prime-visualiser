Prime Explorer — publication edition

Extract this ZIP directly into public_html/apps/prime-visualiser/ and overwrite existing files. index.html, css/, js/ and images/ must sit directly in that folder. Refresh with Ctrl+F5. No build step required.

Includes Ulam spiral, distribution and prime gaps, twin-prime highlights, Clear and PNG export. Zoom animation progressively expands the generated range up to 10,000,000 integers, preserving the centre as each larger field loads. Manual zoom-out also extends the range at the fitted view. The animation is opt-in and applies to the Ulam spiral; Play resumes a paused zoom, Speed changes its duration, Loop repeats the sequence and Reset view restores the fitted view. Switching modes or hiding the tab pauses the animation.

PrimeSieve.js retained unchanged. The Ulam presentation was rebuilt with cached rendering. Graph colours and high-DPI sizing were updated.
